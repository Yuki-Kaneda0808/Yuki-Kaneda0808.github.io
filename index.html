Mammalian reproduction · Epigenetics · Development

Yuki Kaneda

I study how molecular information is established in germ cells and remodeled during early mammalian development.

My work combines mouse genetics, reproductive technologies, embryo manipulation, molecular perturbation, and genome-scale profiling to connect molecular mechanisms with developmental and reproductive phenotypes.
