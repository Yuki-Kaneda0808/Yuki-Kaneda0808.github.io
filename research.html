Research vision

From molecular states to biological inheritance

My central interest is how biological information is encoded in the mammalian germline, remodeled after fertilization, and translated into developmental phenotypes.

01 · DISCOVER

Find the molecular determinants of reproductive function.

Mouse genetics provides a direct route from gene perturbation to sperm structure, fertilization, fertility, and developmental outcome. I use this framework to identify molecular components that are necessary for germ-cell differentiation and function.

02 · RESOLVE

Track epigenetic information through early development.

Fertilization triggers extensive chromatin and DNA methylation remodeling. I am interested in allele-resolved and genome-scale approaches that reveal when parental molecular states are erased, retained, or converted into new developmental states.

03 · ENGINEER

Move from correlation to causality.

The long-term goal is to engineer defined molecular states in vivo and test their consequences across germ cells, embryos, tissues, and generations. This requires linking precise perturbation with rigorous molecular readouts and developmental phenotyping.
